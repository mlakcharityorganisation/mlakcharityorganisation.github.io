Our story

People first. Always.

MLAK Charity Organisation exists to support people and communities facing social and economic challenges. Our approach combines direct assistance, community development, partnerships and opportunities that help people move forward.

As the organisation grows, this website can become your public accountability hub: publishing programmes, policies, reports, safeguarding information, fundraising updates and verified impact.

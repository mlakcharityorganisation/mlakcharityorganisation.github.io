From Miles of Walking to Clean Water at Home

In a remote village in Eastern Uganda, women and young girls spent many hours every day walking long distances under the hot sun just to collect water that was often contaminated.

Children frequently fell sick with waterborne diseases. Mothers had less time for farming or caring for their families.

Thanks to generous donors, Mlak Charity Organization drilled a deep borehole right in the heart of the community. Today, clean and safe water flows freely. The women now have more time for their families, children are healthier, and the entire village has new hope.

This is the power of Sadaqah Jariyah.

Amina’s New Beginning

Amina lost both her parents at a very young age. She was living with relatives who could barely afford food, let alone school fees.

Through our Orphan Sponsorship program, Amina now receives monthly support that covers her education, school uniform, books, nutritious meals, and basic healthcare.

Today she is one of the top students in her class and dreams of becoming a teacher so she can help other children like herself.

Your sponsorship doesn’t just change one life — it changes an entire future.

Building a House of Allah

For many years, the community prayed under a temporary shelter made of iron sheets. During the rainy season, prayers were often interrupted by leaking roofs.

With the support of donors, Mlak Charity Organization constructed a beautiful and solid mosque complete with a prayer hall, ablution area, and a small space for Quran classes.

Now the community gathers in dignity. Children learn the Quran, Friday prayers are full, and the mosque has become the heart of the village.

This is a continuous charity (Sadaqah Jariyah) that will benefit the donors long after they have left this world.
